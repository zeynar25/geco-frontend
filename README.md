As one of the fastest-growing industries worldwide, tourism demands that cities pursue sustainability, and walkability has emerged as a key strategy for promoting health, reducing car use, and enhancing the livability and tourist satisfaction that many car-centered Philippine cities—plagued by traffic congestion, pollution, and unsafe pedestrian areas—still lack. Bonifacio Global City (BGC) stands out with its wide sidewalks and green open spaces, yet local research on how its walkability affects tourist satisfaction remains limited, making this study's exploration of tourist perceptions and their impact on overall visitor experience a timely contribution.
